THE RULES

Plan the exit
before the reward.

01

Harvest

Move your mint token 1 or 2 tiles horizontally or vertically. Collect the destination tile's energy. The central core is worth 8.

02

Close the path

The tile you leave goes dark permanently. You cannot cross dark tiles or jump over the opponent. The middle tile of a two-space move remains available.

03

Win on energy

Turns alternate. If one player is trapped, their turn is skipped. When both are trapped, the higher energy total wins. Equal totals are a draw.

The challenge: a high-value tile can become a dead end. ORBIT looks ahead at both players' moves, rather than choosing randomly.
